Privacy Policy for Mica

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Mica is a personal countdown and year-progress app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it — in short: it stays on your device.

What Mica stores

When you add an event (a birthday, deadline, trip, or anything else you're tracking), Mica saves its title, date, type, color, repeat setting, reminder setting, and any note you add. This is stored in a local database on your phone (SQLite) and never leaves it.

What Mica does not do

  • No account or sign-up is required, and none of your data is tied to an identity.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. Mica has no backend — there is nothing to send it to.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are active in the app.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Permissions Mica requests

  • Notifications — to remind you about upcoming events at the time you choose.
  • Receive boot completed and vibrate — so reminders still fire (with haptic feedback) after your phone restarts.

Mica does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, or media.

Your data, your control

You can delete any event at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Mica removes its local database and everything in it — permanently, since nothing is backed up anywhere else.

Children's privacy

Mica is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, since it collects nothing from its servers — it doesn't have any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of Mica adds optional cloud sync — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.
